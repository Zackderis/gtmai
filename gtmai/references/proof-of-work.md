# Proof-of-work deck recipes (Stage 6a)

Every outreach deck opens with a **free sample of the user's actual work, done on that prospect**. Pick the recipe that matches the service this campaign sells, adapt it to the user's brief, and confirm it with the user once.

## Any other service: the method (use when nothing below fits, e.g. a niche or local service)
1. **The buyer.** Who at the prospect buys this service (title)? Take it from the brief.
2. **The belief.** What is the one thing that buyer must believe after cards 2–5? Usually *"they already understand my situation and have done part of the job"*.
3. **The sample.** What does the user deliver when they start work: an audit, a map, a plan, a count, a comparison? Do a small slice of that, on this prospect, using only:
   - the prospect's public website and pages;
   - web search (news, job posts, reviews, ad libraries, public filings);
   - Apollo counts (free people counts; company counts cost 1 credit; Apollo's agent for market, ICP, intent and tech).
4. **The numbers.** Pick 3–6 data points that are specific to the prospect and verifiable. Each one needs a source. Label estimates, with their method.
5. **The bridge.** Write one card on how the user's service turns that sample into results, then real proof from the brief.
6. **The guardrails:**
   - no active probing or scanning;
   - nothing behind a login;
   - no invented numbers or claims about the prospect;
   - no personal data beyond business contacts.
7. **If the prospect's public data is thin:** do the sample at market level (their industry or region) and say so.

Always show the user the recipe in plain words before the first deck, and adjust it to their feedback.

## AI sales training / sales workshops (Zack Deris, ZDTS)
*Aliases:* AI sales training, sales workshop, AI prospecting training, Claude + Apollo training, sales enablement training, outbound training, ZDTS
**Buyer:** CRO, VP/Head of Sales, Sales Director, Head of Sales Enablement, Head of Sales Development; Founder/CEO at companies under ~50 staff (they are still the sales leader)
**Wow belief:** They already know how to find our customers better than we do, and my team could be doing this every week.
**Free sample:** Your market map

**Data points:**
- **The prospect's ICP in their own words** (free; visual: text card: 'Who you sell to (our read)', 4 lines (industry, size, region, buyer) with a source footnote): Fetch the prospect's homepage (the URL the user gave), then follow links found on it to /customers or /case-studies, industries/solutions pages, pricing and careers. Read 1-2 open AE/SDR job posts (careers page, or LinkedIn/Indeed listings that appear in web search results). Web-search 'prospect G2 reviews' for reviewer segment labels (e.g. 'Mid-Market 51-1000 emp.'); G2 often blocks fetches, so use the snippets. Output: industries, size band, HQ regions and buyer titles, each with a source URL. Then turn it into an explicit filter list (q_organization_keyword_tags, organization_num_employees_ranges, organization_locations, person_titles, person_seniorities) and get the user's OK on those exact values before any count runs. Sanity-check the keyword tags: run the base count with per_page 10 and read the employer names; if more than 3 of 10 don't fit, tighten the tags and re-check.
- **Reachable decision-makers (total ICP buyers)** (free; visual: big-number stat card plus a 2-bar chart (in Apollo vs verified email)): apollo_mixed_people_api_search, per_page 1, reading the top-level total_entries only. Filters: the approved q_organization_keyword_tags, organization_num_employees_ranges (e.g. '51,200','201,500','501,1000'), organization_locations (employer HQ), person_titles, person_seniorities (e.g. 'c_suite','vp','head','director'), contact_email_status=['verified']. Run the same count once without contact_email_status to show 'in Apollo' vs 'with a verified email'.
- **TAM: number of ICP companies and a theoretical revenue ceiling** (apollo credits; visual: stat card: '[N] companies in Apollo match your ICP', with a formula footnote): apollo_mixed_companies_search with the same company filters (q_organization_keyword_tags, organization_num_employees_ranges, organization_locations), per_page 1, reading the total. First say exactly: 'This will consume 1 credit. Do you want to proceed?' and wait for yes. (The free apollo_organizations_lookup does not return a total, so it can't replace this.) Optional value = company count × the prospect's lowest published annual price, in the currency their pricing page uses, labelled 'Theoretical ceiling if every matching company bought your entry plan, not a forecast', with the formula on the card. Leave the value out if pricing isn't public or is per seat with no stated minimum. If the user declines the credit, show only the people count from data point 2.
- **Where the buyers are: seniority, company size and region** (free; visual: bar chart by seniority, bar chart by size band, bar chart of the top 5 regions): Re-run the free count from data point 2, changing one filter per run: one person_seniorities value per run (c_suite / vp / head / director); one organization_num_employees_ranges band per run; and one person_locations value per run for the top 5 regions (person_locations is where the buyer sits; organization_locations is employer HQ, so say which one the chart uses). About 10-15 free counts in total. Bars may not add up to the total because people can match several values; say so in a footnote.
- **Technologies their ICP companies use** (free; visual: horizontal bar chart: % of ICP buyers by tool): Pick 6-10 candidate tools relevant to the prospect's category: tools on their integrations page, competitors' products, and adjacent stack (e.g. salesforce, hubspot, netsuite, shopify, zendesk). Write uids with underscores for spaces and periods. Check each uid first by counting it alone with no other filters; a 0 means the uid is wrong, so try another spelling or drop it. Then re-run the data-point-2 count with currently_using_any_of_technology_uids=[that one tool]. Share = tool count ÷ base count. Label: 'share of ICP decision-makers whose employer uses X (Apollo-detected)'.
- **Buying-intent topics for their ICP** (apollo agent (may ask credits); visual: table: topic | why it signals a buyer for [prospect] | source (Apollo agent / suggested)): Use the Apollo agent (apollo_agent_research_and_analyze). The tool needs the request in the user's own words, so show the user this text and send it only after they confirm: 'For prospect.com: their TAM, ICP, buying intent topics (Bombora topics), technologies their ICP companies use'. Poll apollo_agent_poll_task with the returned cursor until the task completes (it can take minutes). If it asks to spend credits, relay the question to the user word for word and never answer it yourself. Use only the topics the agent returns, and label them the way the agent does (measured intent vs. suggested). If it returns none, make a card called 'Intent topics worth tracking' from the prospect's G2 category and competitor names, labelled 'suggested, not measured'.
- **Who's in market now: trigger counts** (free; visual: bar chart: all ICP buyers vs at growing companies vs at companies hiring for the problem vs at scaling companies): Start from the data-point-2 filters and add one trigger per free count. Growing: organization_headcount_growth_past_n_months=12 with organization_headcount_growth_range {min: 15}. Hiring for the problem: organization_job_posted_at_range {min: today minus 60 days, max: today} as YYYY-MM-DD dates, plus q_organization_job_titles=[roles the prospect's product helps, e.g. 'payroll manager' for payroll software]. Scaling: organization_num_jobs_range {min: 10}.
- **10 sample target accounts** (free; visual: table: account | size band | HQ region | why now (source, date checked)): Run the trigger-filtered people search with per_page 100 (free). Results include only the employer's name (no domain, size or HQ) and masked personal names; ignore the people. Dedupe the employer names. Get each one's domain with the free apollo_organizations_lookup (q_organization_fuzzy_name = company name). Size band and HQ region = the filter values the search used. Pick the 10 best fits, open each one's website or careers page, and write one 'why now' line citing a public source and the date you checked it (e.g. 'SDR role open on careers page, checked 27 Oct').
- **Size of the prospect's own sales team (sizes the workshop)** (free; visual: text): Free count with q_organization_domains_list=['prospect.com'], person_titles=['account executive','sales development representative','business development representative','sales manager','account manager'] and include_similar_titles=false. Add the open sales roles on their careers page. On the offer card write 'about [N] sellers (Apollo-visible, may lag LinkedIn)'. Counts only, no names.

**Deck outline:** [Prospect] × ZDTS: we mapped your market before we met → Who you sell to (our read of your ICP, from your own site and job posts) → Your market in numbers: [N] ICP companies, [M] reachable decision-makers → Where they are: seniority, size and region → What they run and what they're researching (tech + intent topics) → In market now: 10 accounts worth calling this week → Built with Claude + Apollo in [actual time]. Your [N] sellers can do it every week. → The AI Sales Workshop: what your team leaves with (+ real results only) → Next step: 20 minutes to pick your first campaign
**Bridge to offer:** Card 7 pivots: 'Everything on cards 2-6 was built with Claude and Apollo in [actual elapsed time], using [actual credits] Apollo credits; the rest came from free searches. In the workshop your sellers learn to build this map, spot in-market accounts and write the first message themselves, every week.' State the real time and credits from this run, never a stock '30 minutes'. Then the workshop format (live sessions, plus the prompts and skills they keep), sized to the team count from data point 9.
**Guardrails:** Every number carries its source and date, e.g. 'Apollo database count, verified emails only, 27 Oct 2026'. These counts measure Apollo's coverage, not the true size of the market.; The user approves the explicit filter values before any count runs; Apollo's direct search tools need explicit values, not a description. Anything inferred is labelled 'our read of your ICP'.; Show a revenue ceiling only when there's public pricing, always with the formula and the words 'not a forecast'. Otherwise show only counts.; Sample accounts are company names only. No individual names, emails or phone numbers in the deck.; Company search costs 1 credit per count: say exactly 'This will consume 1 credit. Do you want to proceed?' and wait. Relay any Apollo agent credit question word for word and never answer it yourself.; Show intent topics as measured only if the Apollo agent presents them as measured. Otherwise label them 'suggested topics to track'.; Fetch only URLs the user gave, that came from search results, or that are linked on pages already fetched. If a page won't open, give the user the link and ask them to paste what it shows.; Never claim to know their pipeline, quota attainment or win rates.
**If data is thin:** No customers page or job posts: infer the ICP from 2-3 direct competitors' customer pages plus the prospect's pricing page, show two ICP hypotheses side by side, and run the free counts for both. If the keyword tags return fewer than about 200 people, widen the size bands and geography by one step and say so on the card. If the Apollo agent stalls or the user declines its credits, ship the deck without intent topics and offer them as a follow-up.

## Outbound / lead generation agency
*Aliases:* lead gen agency, outbound agency, cold email agency, appointment setting agency, pipeline generation agency, B2B lead generation
**Buyer:** Founder/CEO (seed to Series B), VP/Head of Sales, CRO, Head of Growth, VP Marketing/Demand Gen
**Wow belief:** They've already built our target list and the first campaign. They could start booking meetings next week.
**Free sample:** Your first campaign, pre-built

**Data points:**
- **ICP read** (free; visual: text card: 'Who we'd target (our read)'): Same method as the market map: the homepage the user gave, then linked customers/case-study, industries, pricing and careers pages; AE/SDR job posts; G2 reviewer segments from search snippets. Cite each source. Convert to explicit Apollo filter values and get the user's OK before counting. Sanity-check the keyword tags by reading 10 employer names from a per_page 10 search.
- **Reachable market by campaign segment** (free; visual: bar chart: verified-email decision-makers per segment): Split the approved ICP into 3 segments (by industry keyword, size band or region). For each segment run a free apollo_mixed_people_api_search count (per_page 1, top-level total_entries) with q_organization_keyword_tags, organization_num_employees_ranges, organization_locations, person_titles, person_seniorities and contact_email_status=['verified'].
- **Campaign runway (estimate)** (free; visual: table: segment | verified contacts | weeks of runway at [pace]/week): Verified contacts per segment ÷ a weekly sending pace the user states (ask once if it isn't already in the chat or their Context Center, e.g. 500 new contacts a week) = weeks of runway. Label it an estimate and print the pace used.
- **Act-now tier: accounts with a trigger** (free; visual: grouped bar chart: segment total vs contacts at triggered accounts): Within each segment run free counts that each add one trigger: organization_job_posted_at_range {min: today minus 60 days, max: today} in YYYY-MM-DD plus q_organization_job_titles=[roles that signal the pain]; organization_headcount_growth_past_n_months=6 with organization_headcount_growth_range {min: 20}; organization_num_jobs_range {min: 10}.
- **10 accounts to contact first, each with a why-now** (free; visual: table: account | segment | trigger | source, date checked): Free people search with per_page 100 on the triggered filters. Results show only employer names, so dedupe them and get domains with the free apollo_organizations_lookup (q_organization_fuzzy_name). Verify each account's trigger on its careers page or in a news result, and record the source and the date you checked (careers pages often don't show posting dates, so write 'open on careers page, checked [date]' rather than inventing a post date).
- **Recently funded accounts in their ICP** (free; visual: table: company | round date | amount (only if public) | source): Free apollo_organizations_lookup with the approved company filters plus latest_funding_date_range {min: today minus 6 months, max: today}, per_page 25. It returns names and domains only, and its filters can be loose, so check each candidate against the ICP and confirm the round in a public press release or news article. Show the round amount only if that public source states it. Optional, if the user wants Apollo's own funding fields: apollo_mixed_companies_search with the same filters, after saying exactly 'This will consume 1 credit. Do you want to proceed?'
- **The prospect's current outbound footprint** (free; visual: text card: 'How you generate pipeline today (outside view)'): Free count of SDR/BDR titles with q_organization_domains_list=['prospect.com'] and include_similar_titles=false. Add their open SDR roles from the careers page, the outbound tools their job posts name (Outreach, Salesloft, Apollo, Instantly), and their homepage CTA ('book a demo' vs 'start free'), which shows the sales motion. Describe it neutrally.
- **Sample first-touch messages for 2 segments** (free; visual: text cards (2 email mock-ups)): Claude drafts 2 short openers from the prospect's public value proposition, a real verified trigger from the 10-accounts table, and the segment's pain. No individual recipient is named. Each is marked 'draft, not sent'.

**Deck outline:** [Prospect]: your first outbound campaign, already built → Who we'd target (your ICP, as we read it) → Your reachable market: [N] verified decision-makers in 3 segments → Act-now accounts: hiring, growing or just funded → 10 accounts we'd contact first, and why now → What we'd say: 2 sample openers → How we'd run it: data, copy, sending, booking (30/60/90) → Proof: results for clients like you (only real ones) → Next step: approve the list and we launch in [X] days
**Bridge to offer:** Card 7 says: 'The list, the segments and the angles are done. We handle the sending domains and deliverability, the sequences, the replies, and booking into your calendar.' Tie the weeks of runway from data point 3 to the retainer length, using the stated pace.
**Guardrails:** No guarantees on meetings or revenue. Show projections only if they come from the user's own historical rates that the user supplies; otherwise leave them out.; Sample messages are labelled drafts and are never sent.; Company names only. No personal emails, phone numbers or names of individual contacts in the deck.; Every Apollo count is dated and labelled 'verified-email contacts in Apollo'.; Each company search needs the exact sentence 'This will consume 1 credit. Do you want to proceed?' and a yes.; Don't promise sending volumes beyond Google/Yahoo published bulk-sender rules; compliance with GDPR/PECR/CAN-SPAM is the user's process to describe, not something the deck asserts.; Fetch only URLs from the user, search results or links on fetched pages; otherwise ask the user to paste.
**If data is thin:** If the ICP is unclear, run the free counts for two hypotheses and let the prospect pick on the call. If each segment has fewer than about 300 verified contacts, merge them into one segment and widen the geography. If no triggers turn up, lead with ICP fit and headcount growth only.
